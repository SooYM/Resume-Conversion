# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who already have a resume in another file format and need to restyle it into the supplied Resume Conversion instructor resume format. This audience and workflow are inferred from the explicit brief.

## Product Purpose

Convert an uploaded resume into the supplied one-page Resume Conversion instructor template, surface missing attributes, let the user complete or correct every field, and export the result as PDF.

## Positioning

The supplied `Resumes_Template.pdf` is the binding output contract rather than an optional theme.

## Operating Context

Users upload a PDF, DOCX, text, rich-text, HTML, Markdown, or image resume; review best-effort extracted data; fill missing values; optionally add a portrait; and print or save the formatted A4 page as PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Automatic extraction is best-effort and always requires user review.
- Missing or unrecognized attributes remain blank and editable.
- Unknown file types still open a blank template instead of blocking the user.
- The original template is one A4 page; long content may continue to additional printed pages to prevent data loss.
- No account, database, or cloud storage is required. Files stay in the browser.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the bilingual Resume Conversion instructor heading, field order, education table, numbered working-experience layout, portrait placement, and restrained black-on-white document style from the supplied PDF.

## Evidence on Hand

- Source template: `/Users/sooyauming/Downloads/Resumes_Template.pdf`
- Extracted template text and rendered inspection image live under `tmp/pdfs/`.
- No testimonials, pricing, customer claims, or deployment requirements were supplied and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Template fidelity over visual novelty.
- Never hide extraction uncertainty; every value stays editable.
- Preserve user data rather than forcing one-page truncation.
- Keep resume contents local to the browser.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, clear status messaging, labelled fields, and reduced-motion support are required.
